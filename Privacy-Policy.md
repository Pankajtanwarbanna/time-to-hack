**Privacy Policy**  
Effective Date: 3rd May 2025  

**Data Collection**  
Time To Hack does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The extension operates entirely within your browser and analyzes the strength of passwords typed into password fields on websites. No user data, login credentials, or browsing history is collected, stored, or transmitted to any external server.

**Usage**
When you use Time To Hack:
- The extension analyzes passwords locally in your browser to estimate the crack time under various attack scenarios.
- No password data is sent to external servers for processing.
- The extension does not store or retain any analyzed data after it has been processed.

**Third-Party Services**
Time To Hack does not rely on third-party services for password analysis. All password strength calculations are performed locally within the extension using the zxcvbn algorithm, an open-source password strength estimator.

**Permissions Justification**
- **storage**: Used to store user preferences, such as selected attack scenarios or custom settings.
- **host permissions (all websites)**: Necessary to process password data on all websites you visit.

**Security Measures**
- The extension does not execute remote code or store external scripts.
- All password strength analysis is done locally on your browser.
- No user data, passwords, or browsing history is shared or transmitted outside of your device.
